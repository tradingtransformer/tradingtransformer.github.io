The reasoning

Spain to lift the trophy. Spain's Euro 2024 title was not a stylistic accident. Their ball-control tactics — short cycles, high pass counts, patient pressing — travel especially well in warm-weather conditions, where opponents tire and turnovers get punished. A North American summer, with midday kickoffs and long recovery windows between group stages and knockouts, was the ideal environment for a possession-first side. Everyone else was going to overheat. Spain was built to keep the ball and let the sun do the rest.

Mbappé to finish as top scorer. The Messi–Mbappé question at this World Cup was, in the end, about age. Across seven high-intensity matches in a compressed schedule, the younger player's recovery advantage compounds every round. Mbappé was still in his physical peak; Messi, however brilliant, was carrying more mileage into every knockout. Over a grueling bracket, that gap tells.
